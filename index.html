02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Growing ideas.
Making connections.

Coimbatore, India → Madison, Wisconsin

I'm Raagav Vijayalakshmi Siva, an Agricultural Business Management student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, with certificates in Entrepreneurship, Consulting, and Textile & Design.

Originally from Coimbatore, India, I'm passionate about sustainable agriculture, consumer brands, and building businesses that create meaningful impact.

An entrepreneur at heart, I founded Aadhiyan Farms, pioneering indoor microgreens production in Coimbatore under the brand Lush Greens, and later launched Migai, a platform connecting artisanal food brands with local consumers. My internships at Akshayakalpa Organic and Organic Valley took me into agricultural business models, commercial strategy, and data-driven decision-making.

I'm also an undergraduate researcher in UW–Madison's Department of Bacteriology, exploring natural fungal fermentates for food preservation and mycotoxin mitigation. I'm a recipient of the Sophomore Research Fellowship and Renk Scholarship, and a participant in the Farm Foundation Cultivators program.

Outside academics, I'm a passionate mridangam player, with interests in art, design, travel, and storytelling.

Always curious. Always growing.
